About Me

I am an Associate Professor at the College of Life Science and Technology, Huazhong University of Science and Technology. My research interests include readout electronics for medical imaging devices, brain computer interface, brain sensing and stimulation, ultrasonic imaging, high-performance reconfigurable hardware, ultra-fast signal sampling and processing, mobile sensing, deep learning, signal processing, and embedded system design.

Research Goals

  • Brain Sensing and Stimulation
  • Earable sensing
  • Ultrasonic sensing for medical and industrial applications
  • Multi-channel high-speed data acquisition systems
